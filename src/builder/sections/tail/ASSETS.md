## Wave 4 (2026-11-07)

- **Blender models: 44 files in `public/assets/models/builder/tail/`**, all our own work.
  - Every mesh and texture is generated by the Python scripts in `src/builder/tools/blender/tail/`, from real dimensions
    and proportions: `engine.py`, `thrust.py`, `boosters.py`, `gear.py` and the shared `tl.py`.
  - The textures (cooling tubes, corrugated skin, quilted blankets, Starship's hexagonal tiles) are drawn by formula
    with numpy. No photo was traced and no third-party model was cut or reused.
  - No licence or credit line is needed. The project licence applies.
- **Free models we did not use:** `redstone.glb`, `atlas.glb` and NASA's "Space Launch System Block 1" (public domain)
  were checked for proportions only.
- **Reference photos.** The research photos come from images.nasa.gov and stay outside the repo.
  - Most are NASA photos and in the public domain.
  - `electron_pad.jpg` (a Rocket Lab photo on the NASA site) and `proton_zvezda.jpg` (no credit given) are reference
    only: look and measure, never ship or trace.
