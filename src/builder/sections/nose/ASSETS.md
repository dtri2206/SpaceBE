# Nose section: assets

## Wave 4 (2026-11-07): Blender models, all our own

65 nose parts use a Blender model (`"model": { "builder": "nose/<slug>" }`, files in
`public/assets/models/builder/nose/`, 5.3 MB in all). Every mesh and texture is generated by the scripts in
`src/builder/tools/blender/nose/` (`build.py` builds them all; `models.json` there lists each model's part, size,
triangles and nodes): our own geometry from real dimensions, and textures computed with numpy (shingles, quilted
blankets, tiles, solar cells) from fixed formulas. No third-party mesh, scan or texture is shipped, so there is nothing
to credit. The Smithsonian CC0 scans and the NASA 3D Resources models listed in the research were not needed.

Reference photos and drawings were used only to measure and compare (the wave-4 research folder lists them with their
licences in `refs/LICENSES.md`); none is shipped or traced into a texture. Images marked REFONLY or CAUTION (Shenzhou,
Gaganyaan, Long March 5, PSLV, Electron, Ariane 5, the Crew Dragon drawing, Starliner and Crew Dragon press photos) were
looked at only.

## Models the nose no longer uses

These were the art of capsule_s / capsule_m / capsule_l and antenna_low / medium / high; the nose now draws those parts itself.
No other file in `src/` references them, so they can join the unused-model cleanup (or stay for later use):

- stardust_capsule.glb (2.2 MB)
- esas_crew_module.glb (7 KB)
- voyager_antenna.glb (166 KB)
- satkit_radio1.glb (8 KB). satkit_radio2.glb is no longer used by the nose but the body still uses it.

The NASA vehicle models suggested for cutting pieces (redstone.glb, atlas.glb, saturn_v.glb, gemini.glb, apollo_soyuz.glb)
were inspected but not used: redstone.glb and atlas.glb hold only the Mercury capsule (Freedom 7, Friendship 7) with no
escape tower, and saturn_v.glb, gemini.glb and apollo_soyuz.glb are single merged meshes that cannot be cut by node.
